Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026

This policy explains how Chomas Inc. ("we," "us") handles information collected through this website, our consulting services, and the internal software tools we operate.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. When you email us, we receive your name, email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.
  • Client information. During an engagement, we receive business contact details and the information needed to deliver services and send invoices.

This website has no forms, cookies, analytics or tracking.

Website hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Like most web services, these providers may automatically log technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. That information is handled under their own privacy policies (GitHub, Google). We do not receive or use it.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiries and schedule calls
  • To prepare proposals, deliver services and send invoices
  • To keep business and tax records required by law

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Internal applications and connected services

We operate private software tools for internal use only, including applications that connect to third-party services such as the Oura API. These applications:

  • Access only the account of the person who explicitly authorizes the connection, and only the data types that person approves
  • Store retrieved data and access tokens locally on that person's own computer, not on our servers or any third-party server
  • Use the data solely for that person's own personal analysis and dashboards
  • Never sell, share, publish or use the data for advertising, and never combine it with data about anyone else

Access can be revoked at any time from the connected service's account settings. Revoking access stops all further data retrieval, and the locally stored data can be deleted by its owner at any time.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run the business (such as email, accounting and payment processing providers), when required by law, or with your consent.

Client confidential information

Confidential business information you share during an engagement is protected under the terms of your agreement with us and is used only to provide the services.

Retention

We keep correspondence and client records for as long as needed to provide services and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing chomasinc@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, and we will honor those requests as required.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the new version here and change the effective date above.
